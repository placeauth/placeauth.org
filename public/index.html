01 / WHY THIS EXISTS

Places define conditions. Machines need a common language.

Hospitals, warehouses, campuses, homes, and roadways each carry local rules. Today those requirements are often trapped in signage, procedures, or proprietary system internals. PlaceAuth gives the place a machine-readable way to express what must be true.

02 / FROM PERMISSION TO ADMISSION

One question. Six action families.

Authorization asks whether an action is allowed. Admission asks whether an autonomous system can operate here under stated conditions, backed by evidence. The normative SPP 0.1 specification keeps that exchange deliberately small and composable.

03 / READ THE SPEC

Requirements are readable by people and machines.

PlaceAuth connects requirements, conformance, evidence, and operating profiles. Decisions remain explicit, short-lived, and inspectable.

03 / ARCHITECTURE

Three layers, one interoperability contract.

SPP complements identity, fleet coordination, navigation, policy engines, and building systems. It defines the place-facing exchange.

SPP CORE

Spatial requirements

  • place policy semantics
  • PERMIT / DENY / CONDITIONAL
  • hierarchy and inheritance
SPP CONFORMANCE · EXPERIMENTAL

Demonstrate conformance

  • conformance plans
  • requirement-to-proof mapping
  • behavioral and attested evidence
  • assurance levels
SPP ADMISSION · EXPERIMENTAL

Establish an operating profile

  • ADMITTED / DEGRADED / DENIED
  • RequirementDelta
  • selective requalification

SPP is designed to integrate with existing robot and building infrastructure; it does not replace those systems.

04 / SELECTIVE REQUALIFICATION

When the place changes, the proof can adapt.

Hierarchical spaces let a machine reuse valid evidence and run only the tests introduced by a changed boundary or requirement set.

ABOUT PLACEAUTH FOUNDATION

The layer between machines and places.

PlaceAuth Foundation is developing experimental open infrastructure intended to help machines and places communicate operating requirements, evidence, and admissible conditions across vendor boundaries.

SPP does not inherently force a malicious system to comply. Trust depends on evidence assurance, runtime integrity, enforcement points, site infrastructure, and attestation or observation. The preview is not production security infrastructure.
